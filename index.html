Hoshimachi Suisei

Virtual Idol & Singer ☄️

Profile

Hoshimachi Suisei (星街すいせい) is a Japanese virtual singer and YouTuber who calls herself “your shooting star, your diamond in the rough.” She began on YouTube as an independent VTuber on March 22, 2018, and later joined hololive Production as a member of its 0th generation.

Music is at the center of her career. Besides covers, she writes and releases original songs, and her albums include Still Still Stellar (2021) and Specter (2023). She is also known for her gaming streams and for her competitive Tetris skills.

Milestones

  1. March 22, 2018 – Debuts on YouTube as an independent VTuber
  2. March 1, 2020 – Debuts her 3D model
  3. June 26, 2021 – Her channel reaches 1 million subscribers

Fast Facts

  • Birthday: March 22 (the same day as her debut)
  • Fan name: Hoshiyomi
  • Signature mark: ☄️ (comet)
